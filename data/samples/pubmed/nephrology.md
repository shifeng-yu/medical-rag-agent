## Belimumab for Acute Kidney Injury: A Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis

**PMID:** 160000000 | **Year:** 2024

### Background
Acute Kidney Injury represents a significant cause of morbidity and mortality worldwide, affecting millions of patients annually. Current standard therapies provide incomplete symptom control and disease modification in many patients. Belimumab has emerged as a promising therapeutic option based on its mechanism of action targeting key pathophysiological pathways involved in acute kidney injury.

### Methods
We conducted a systematic review and meta-analysis involving 2,500 participants with confirmed acute kidney injury (mean age 60 years, 58% male). Participants were randomized to receive either belimumab or standard care. The primary endpoint was disease progression. Secondary endpoints included safety outcomes, patient-reported quality of life, and subgroup analyses by age, sex, and disease severity. Median follow-up was 6 months.

### Results
Treatment with belimumab resulted in a 37% relative improvement in disease progression versus standard care (HR 0.86, 95% CI 0.73-0.76, p<0.001). The absolute risk reduction was 26 percentage points (NNT = 9). Benefits were consistent across all prespecified subgroups including age, sex, and baseline disease severity. Adverse events were reported in 24% of the treatment group versus 14% of controls, most commonly mild and self-limiting. Serious adverse events occurred in 5% and 5% respectively.

### Conclusions
Belimumab demonstrates clinically meaningful and statistically significant efficacy for acute kidney injury, with an acceptable safety profile. These findings support the incorporation of belimumab into evidence-based treatment algorithms for appropriately selected patients. Further research is needed to evaluate long-term durability and identify predictive biomarkers.
